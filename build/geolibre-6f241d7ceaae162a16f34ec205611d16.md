# GeoLibre

**English** · [Bahasa Indonesia](id/geolibre.md)

GeoLibre, by [Qiusheng Wu](https://github.com/giswqs) ([opengeos](https://github.com/opengeos/GeoLibre)), is an open-source GIS that runs in the browser. You can run it right here, including installing my plugins from its marketplace.

## About GeoLibre

[GeoLibre](https://geolibre.app) is not my software. It is created and maintained by [Qiusheng Wu](https://github.com/giswqs) ([ORCID 0000-0001-5437-4073](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5437-4073)), University of Tennessee, Knoxville, under the [opengeos](https://github.com/opengeos) organisation, and released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/opengeos/GeoLibre/blob/main/LICENSE). I contribute plugins to its ecosystem (see [Plugins](plugins.md)).

GeoLibre is a free, cloud-native GIS that runs in the browser, on the desktop, on mobile, and in Jupyter notebooks, with 1,000+ geoprocessing tools running on WebAssembly. It is built with [Tauri](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri), [React](https://github.com/facebook/react), [TypeScript](https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript), [MapLibre GL JS](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js), [DuckDB-WASM](https://github.com/duckdb/duckdb-wasm), and [deck.gl](https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl).
