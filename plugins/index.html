About GeoLibre¶

GeoLibre is not my software. It is created and maintained by Qiusheng Wu (ORCID 0000-0001-5437-4073), University of Tennessee, Knoxville, under the opengeos organisation, and released under the MIT License. I contribute plugins to its ecosystem (the plugins on this page).

GeoLibre is a free, cloud-native GIS that runs in the browser, on the desktop, on mobile, and in Jupyter notebooks, with 1,000+ geoprocessing tools running on WebAssembly. It is built with Tauri, React, TypeScript, MapLibre GL JS, DuckDB-WASM, and deck.gl.
